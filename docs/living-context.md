# Living Coach context

The backend may supply optional `living_context` version1 to ordinary Kata.fit
chat and typed-generation acquisition. It is account-owned or independently
Dojo-audience-authorized backend data, not worker-local memory, instructions or
an action grant. The worker validates its closed shape and16KiB UTF-8 bound,
preserves immediate evidence/targets and tools, and removes only exactly repeated
historical snippets already present in the conversation. Missing, unsupported or
malformed optional reports are omitted rather than blocking generation.

Use the dated report for routine continuity; use ordinary authorized tools for
additional detail, safety uncertainties and fresh checks. Current trigger and
canonical current-day evidence override older report text. Withheld/partial
history is unknown, never zero intake or evidence of completed work.

The new backend typed `living_context` field requires a coordinated worker update;
older installed-worker compatibility was explicitly waived for this rollout.
The backend operations and source/publication contract are documented in its
`regimen-backend/docs/living-coach-context.md`. This change adds no new UI,
credential, sharing grant or model-provider requirement.
